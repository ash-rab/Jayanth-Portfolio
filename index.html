About

The Person Behind
the Work

I'm Jayanth S Kumar — a creative professional based in Bangalore, working across brand identity, marketing strategy, motion, and experience design.

My work spans from developing the complete visual identity for Cargo Ville — a container-café concept — to crafting strategic marketing analyses for global brands like Red Bull. I believe the best creative work sits at the intersection of rigorous thinking and strong aesthetics.

When I'm not designing, I'm documenting moments through video and building content for social — always curious, always experimenting at the edge of new tools and ideas.
